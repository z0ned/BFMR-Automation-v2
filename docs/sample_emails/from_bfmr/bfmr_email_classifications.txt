1. Order & Shipment Activity (Personal / Transactional)
Emails tracking individual packages and shipments sent to BFMR:
Tracking Submitted / Noted: Confirmations that you submitted a retailer tracking number (e.g., TBA..., 1Z...) to the BFMR dashboard.
Deal Subscriptions: Notifications confirming you claimed or reserved a specific item to buy.
Package Status Updates: Notices regarding the intake status of your shipments (e.g., "Package Received", "Set Aside").

2. Payments & Financials (Personal / Transactional)
Direct financial records for completed transactions:
Payment Sent (ACH / Bank): Notifications detailing itemized payouts, payment amounts, and tracking numbers sent to your bank account.
Crypto / Instant Payouts: Notices confirming payment transfers in Bitcoin, USDC, or other stablecoins.

3. Customer Support & Inquiry Tickets (Interactive)
Correspondence regarding issues with specific shipments, deals, or account queries:
Support Ticket Created / Acknowledged: Automated receipts when an inquiry is submitted (e.g., Ticket #150844, #126998).
Direct Support Conversations: Back-and-forth dialogue regarding order discrepancies, return labels, duplicate deal listings, or missing unit numbers.
Ticket Resolution & Feedback Requests: Surveys confirming a ticket has been closed (e.g., "Resolved. How did we do?").

4. Deal Alerts & Payout Broadcasts (Marketing / Buyer Alerts)
General alerts regarding buying opportunities posted by BFMR:
New Deals / In-Stock Alerts: Announcements of new items eligible for purchase (e.g., PlayStation 5, Apple Watch Series 11, RTX 5080, Fire TV Sticks).
Payout Increases: Notifications that BFMR has increased its buying price ("Above Retail" payout increases).
Deal Deadlines & Closings: Warnings that a deal is shutting down or final cut-off deadlines for drop-offs/deliveries (e.g., "CLOSING - Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max").
Major Shopping Events: Event-specific guides and deal floods (e.g., Amazon Prime Big Deal Days, Fall Deals).

5. Account Security, Authentication & Dev Operations
Technical, administrative, and security-related emails:
2FA & Logins: One-time passcodes and recovery code alerts ("2SV Recovery Code", "Logged In With Recovery Code").
Identity Verification (KYC): Mandatory compliance notices (e.g., Persona account verification announcements).
Developer / Bot Setup: Telegram Bot Father tokens (BFMR_auto_bot) and GitHub SSH key alerts for automated buying scripts (bfmr-automation).

6. Operations, Platform Updates & Policies
Broad operational notices affecting how you do business with BFMR:
Platform Changelogs & New Features: Website feature rollouts, bug fixes, and API upgrades ("✨SITE IMPROVEMENTS✨").
Logistics & Holiday Schedules: Banking cutoffs, extended warehouse drop-off hours, and holiday payment schedules.
Service & Policy Changes: Requirements for physical receipts, updates to BFMR package insurance coverage, and drop-off facility rules.
Referral Promotions: Announcements regarding referral bonuses (e.g., "Double Referral Bonus: $500").
Optional Metadata Tags for Enhanced Filtering

If you are planning to feed these into a spreadsheet or database, you could also assign secondary tags:
Retailer: Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, Apple, Target, Costco, etc.
Product Category: Apple/iPhones, Gaming Consoles, Laptops/Computers, Smart Home/Wearables.
Urgency/Action Required: High (2FA codes, closing deadlines, receipt requests) vs. Low/Informational (general newsletter, payout increase).
